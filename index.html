About

A practice built on trust, preparation and results

Advocate Kumari JayShree practises from Durgapur, West Bengal, appearing before the High Court and District Courts. She advises and represents clients in civil and criminal litigation, corporate and commercial disputes, family law, property and constitutional matters.

Her approach combines careful legal research with practical strategy. Every client receives a candid assessment, a clear plan and regular updates, so no one is left uncertain about where their matter stands.
